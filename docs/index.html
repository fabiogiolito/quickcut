Save clips and edit videos

QuickCut is a tiny Mac app for quick video edits. Open a video, cut it at the playhead, delete the parts you don’t want and drag the rest into order.

Save the whole edit, or right-click any clip to save it as its own file. Nothing is re-encoded, so even long 4K videos save almost instantly.
