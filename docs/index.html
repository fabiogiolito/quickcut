Open. Cut. Delete. Save.

QuickCut is the simple, quick way to edit videos and cut clips on your Mac. None of the timeline-editor complexity.

Native SwiftUI app · macOS 15 or later

    Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

    ⌘B

    Cut at the playhead

    Play your video and press ⌘B wherever you want a cut. It splits right there, even while it plays.

    ⌫

    Delete the bad bits

    Click a segment to select it (⌘ or ⇧-click for more), then press ⌫. Gone.

    ⌘←

    Drag to reorder

    Drop a segment onto another to move it, or nudge the selected one with ⌘← and ⌘→.

    ⌘S

    Saves in a blink

    Your edit is saved as “name edit.mov” next to the original. Nothing is re-encoded, so even long 4K videos save almost instantly.

    ⌘O

    A whole batch at once

    Open several videos and they line up in the sidebar. Switch between them with ⌘[ and ⌘].

    Right-click

    Right-click for the rest

    Rename a video or a clip, save the whole video, or save just one clip as its own file.

    Build it in a minute

    QuickCut is built from source. You’ll need macOS 15 or later and Xcode.

    Terminal
    git clone https://github.com/fabiogiolito/quickcut.git
cd quickcut
./build.sh && open QuickCut.app

    Then drag QuickCut.app into your Applications folder.
